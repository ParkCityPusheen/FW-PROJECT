Dain is absent from most of his duties the day following his conversation, for lack of a better word, with Violet.

Cath doesn't encourage this behavior per se, but it's evident his Swordtail is satisfied with his rider being room-bound while emotionally volatile.

He sleeps in the morning after, if you can call the two hours he manages to actually disassociate enough not to think, sleep, and ignores the soft knocking at his door when Eleni swings by to check on him. Part of him regrets not opening up and pulling her inside, he could've easily propositioned her, and successfully if her demeanor towards him was being perceived accurately. He might've only been with two or three other people his entire life, but he was a good flirt. If there was alcohol especially, but he didn't necessarily need it to initiate anything.

Still, sex could only solve certain problems for particular people.

For Dain it only ever left him feeling raw and exposed and there was nothing he hated more than feeling his skin against another person's and waking up alone and cold in the sheets afterwards. He could go for something strong and sweet though; mead or wine, anything could be fine. His Dad used to come home after patrols or duty irritated and would coerce him to drink with him, complaining about who did this or didn't do that, forcing Dain to listen and agree, pressing the issues so hot against his son's skin they'd eventually become Dain's too.

Currently, he hides underneath his sheets from the sunlight that infiltrates his barracks room through his cushion-sized window. He's tired, like he had been returning from Draithus. He squeezes his pillow closer to him; it would be nice if he could curl up against Cath, but the Red hadn't spoken since voicing his reluctant concern for Tairn. Dain's made to imagine the feeling of smooth and warm red scales and the reverberating pulse behind it.

Skin or scale, he reckons.

Dain drowses off again minutes later and is roused awake by more knocking; this time the wingleader rises to answer it.

To his bemusement Sloane Mairi stands at the threshold of his barracks room looking remarkably neat and distinguished, she tilts her head up to meet his despaired gaze and softens a bit. Dain ignores that, pretends he doesn't notice it, and also looks past the tray trapped in her grip,

"Mairi." The sound of his own gravelly voice makes Dain clear his throat; he tries again after swallowing and Sloane's eyes follow the rise and fall of his laryngeal prominence, "Did you need me?" he frowns when his voice still husks out gruffly.

Sloane fidgets with the tray, and looks away, but then her eyes are on him again.

"I have orders to bring you your meals."

Dain wants to roll his eyes, "On whose orders?"

"Lieutenant Colonel Sorrengail's."

"Did he happen to mention why?"

Sloane shifts her weight along her hips, "You didn't take the food from me."

He presses the flat of his fingers against his beard and rubs, then steps aside; it's too early to argue. He doesn't have the energy to match her.

"I get that I was being combative." she huffs, passing by him when he pulls the door open completely and steps aside to let her in.

He catches her eyes as she goes, but there only emotions he doesn't understand her well enough to translate for himself. He's also fully and appropriately clothed, clad in the soft, dark colored fabrics he's always preferred to sleep in. Still, he looks towards his bare feet to make sure his pants are hugging his hips properly.

"How long are the orders in effect for?" He mutters as she unloads the food onto his table. She avoids answering until all of his meal and, *Loial's mercy,* a carafe of coffee, is neatly organized and off the tray.

"Until Cath stops requesting it." Sloane says when she finally answers

This. *Fucking.* Dragon.

Of course he can't answer to his name in their bond, but can still force a meal to appear. Impeccably parent-like and invasive, as usual.

Dain rounds the table and Sloane to slink back into his bed, "Don't do this again." he orders, "I'm not invalid, but thank you anyway."

"What happened to your back?"

He pauses; the first-year's words take seconds to piece against each other, but as they do he swivels around. It's too late, though, and Sloane's eyes are glimmering in the sunlight that shines perfectly against where she's chosen to stand,

"What happened to your back?" she hisses, though the question is rehashed from her pouty lips with affliction he'd never heard from her before.

"Nothing." He insists, unwilling to entertain the conversation and slipping back into his sheets. He pulls them over his shoulders; there, done. Nothing to see.

"You were burned?" Sloane whispers, "But you just walked away—I hadn't noticed you got hurt."

"Mairi, stop."

Dain twists to face her, "It's not a big deal, it healed. It's fine."

"But it's not; I don't understand how I never noticed——"

"——you don't notice a lot of things." Dain reassures her, "Waste that concern on your assessments and not my skin."

She hadn't noticed he allowed Violet to tamper with her opponents during the days that were her designated matches, turning a blind eye because it had been necessary. He'd gone as far as to arrange benefits for cadets to purposefully lose to her in the most believable ways possible when he and Violet were gone away in the Isles. Sloane would always get Archives Duty on days Jessinia was also on duty. She was never assigned to patrol with cadets Dain considered reckless or loyalists, inexperienced, or particularly hostile toward her. Even now, and he hadn't done so in the last fortnight, her weapons and protective equipment received more frequent inspections than she'd noticed; Sloane chalked that up to him being an annoying bastard more than anything probably.

She assumes a stance that is an uncanny cross between Violet and Imogen; bold and brazen, fists tight at her sides, and her sunlight strands pulled back into a neat bun. Her down-turned lips have a slight gap between them that makes Sloane look like she's perpetually pouting, but the heat in those cerulean lakes she drowns him in, tells him otherwise.

"Why didn't you get it mended?"

Dain feels his mouth go dry, and turns to face the wall once more, "Thank you for breakfast. You're. Dismissed."

"When are you coming to class? It's only eight-hundred."

He doesn't answer, so Sloane speaks into the silence.

"You don't skip class."

"Mairi," Dain groans, "Get *out.*"

She stands there, fidgeting and glancing around as he turns his back to her.

He doesn't dare roll over to watch her go, but he counts the seconds it takes her to shut the door, and then the minutes it takes him to lull back into sleep. His muscles feel heavy as stones and his eyelids somehow heavier as he sinks into black. Dain wonders if Cath will let him roam the realms of his mind and quickly finds out that, *no,* he will not be dreaming on his own tonight. They're all but snuffed out, barely allowed to conceptualize when Cath presses him into a realm of subconsciousness that coincides with his Red's own.

They mix together in the bond. Eyes, ears, and mind; even the privacy of sleep is surrendered to unfamiliar voices and borrowed sights. Dain catches bits and pieces of Cath's conversations, glimpses of peaks and herds of furry cows, smells their char as his Swordtail enjoys a meal upon an undisclosed peak. He spies the scales of other Reds nearby his own, and attempts to get a better look at them but he can't control what he's seeing.

Though, it doesn't take long for his perspective to flicker from mauled bovine to strawberry scales as Cath obliges his tugging quietly. He's out with Thoirt again, Thoirt and Sawyer's bond and... Aotrom and Andarna? His view cuts out as quickly as it'd phased in, and Dain is staring at the bloodied spot where Cath had caught and consumed three cows at once. He squirms in the bond, tucking himself deep into the reserves of their magic.

Andarna and Aotrom are new, two dragons he'd maybe seen three or four times total each in these shared dreams. There's chatter, but none of it is in Cath's sharp voice. There's words to be deciphered between Cath's chewing and chomping but Dain figures he's being sent on his way as the chattering fades and is replaced by nothing.

He sleeps.
