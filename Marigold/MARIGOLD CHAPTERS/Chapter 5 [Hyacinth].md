Dain is absent from most of his duties the day following his conversation, for lack of a better word, with Violet.

Cath doesn't encourage this behavior per se, but it's evident his Swordtail is satisfied with his rider being roombound while emotionally viotale.

He sleeps in the morning after, if you can call the two hours he manages to actually disassociate enough not to think, sleep, and ignores the soft knocking at his door when Eleni swings by to check on him. Part of him regrets not opening up and pulling her inside, he could've easily propositioned her, and successfully if her demeanor towards him was being perceived accurately. He might've only been with two or three other people his entire life, but he was a good flirt. If there was alcohol especially, but he didn't necessarily need it to initiate anything.

Still, sex could only solve certain problems for particular people.

For Dain it only ever left him feeling raw and exposed and there was nothing he hated more than feeling his skin against another person's and waking up alone and cold in the sheets afterwards. He could go for something strong and sweet though; mead or wine, anything could be fine. His Dad used to come home after patrols or duty irritated and would coerce him to drink with him, complaining about who did this or didn't do that, forcing Dain to listen and agree, pressing the issues so hot against his son's skin they'd eventually become Dain's too.

Currently he hides underneath his dark sheets from the sunlight that infiltrates his barracks room through the cushion-sized window. He's tired, like he had been returning from Draithus, only emotionally he recognizes that he's jaded from feeling to the extent that he'd allowed himself prior. He squeezes his pillow closer to him; it would be nice if he could curl up against Cath, but his dragon hadn't spoken to him since his comment regarding Sgaeyl so he's made to imagine the feeling of smooth and warm red scales and the reverberating pulse behind it.

Skin or scale, he reckons.

He drowses off successfully again minutes later to more knocking; this time the wingleader rises to answer it.

"Good morning,"

To his bemusement Sloane Mairi stands at the threshold of his barracks room looking remarkably neat and distinguished, she tilts her head up to meet his despaired gaze and softens a bit. Dain ignores that, pretends he doesn't notice it, and also looks past the tray trapped in her grip,

"Mairi." The sound of his own gravelly voice makes Dain clear his throat; he tries again after swallowing and Sloane's eyes follow the rise and fall of his laryngeal prominence.

"Did you need me?" he frowns when his voice still husks out gruffly.

Sloane fidgets with the tray, and looks away, but then her eyes are on him again.

"I have orders to bring you your meals because I failed to accomplish it initially, after your mending."

Dain wants to roll his eyes, "Whose orders?"

"Lieutenant Colonel Sorrengail's."

He presses the flat of his fingers against his beard and rubs, then steps aside; it's too early to argue. He doesn't have the energy to match her.

"How long are the orders in effect for?" He mutters carefully as she unloads the food onto his table.

Sloane avoids answering until all of it, a total of three things and *Loial's mercy* a carafe of coffee, is neatly organized and off the tray,

"Until Cath stops requesting it." she says when she finally answers

This. Fucking. Dragon. He can't answer a call in their bond but he can still force a meal to appear. Impeccably parent-like and invasive, as per usaual.

Dain rounds the table and Sloane to slink back into his bed, "Don't do this again." he orders, "I'm not invalid, but thank you anyway."

"What happened to your back?"

Dain pauses; the first-year's words take seconds to piece against each other, but as they do he swivels around. It's too late, though, and Sloane's eyes are glimmering in the sunlight that shines perfectly against where she's chosen to stand,

"Did that happen because of me?" her mouth gapes open, astonished, but somehow she looks bothered by it. Dain allows his brows to pinch together

"I'm fine." He insists, unwilling to entertain the conversation and slipping back into his sheets. He pulls them over his shoulders; there, done. Nothing to see.

"You were burned?" Sloane whispers at first before her voice raises with concern, "You had just walked away—I hadn't noticed you got hurt."

"Mairi, *stop.*"

Dain twists to face her, "It's not a big deal, it healed, you're alive, it's fine."

"It's not; I don't understand how I never noticed."

"You don't notice a lot of things." Dain reassures her.

The same way she hadn't noticed he allowed Violet to tamper with her opponents during the days that were her designated matches. He'd gone as far as to arrange benefits for cadets to purposefully lose to her in the most believable ways possible when he and Violet were gone away in the Isles. Sloane would always get Archives Duty on days Jessinia was also on duty. She was never assigned to patrol with cadets Dain considered reckless or loyalists, inexperienced, or particularly hostile toward her. Even now, and he hadn't done so in the last fortnight, her weapons and protective equipment received more frequent inspections than she'd noticed; Sloane chalked that up to him being an annoying bastard more than anything probably.

She assumes a stance that is an uncanny cross between Violet and Imogen; bold and brazen, fists tight at her sides, and her sunlight strands pulled back into a neat bun. Her down-turned lips have a slight gap between them that makes Sloane look like she's perpetually pouting, but the heat in those cerulean lakes she drowns him in, tells him otherwise.

"Why didn't you get it mended?"

Dain feels his mouth go dry, "Thank you for breakfast. You're. Dismissed."

"When are you coming to class? It's only eight-hundred."

"I might not."

"You don't skip class."

"Mairi," Dain groans, "Get *out.*"

She stands there, fidgeting and glancing around as he turns his back to her.

He doesn't dare roll over to watch her go, but he counts the seconds it takes her to shut the door, and then the minutes it takes him to lull back into sleep.
