Dain is absent from most of his duties the day following his conversation, for lack of a better word, with Violet.

He's emotionally void proceeding that whole "discussion" too; he sleeps in the morning after, if you can call the two hours he strains away into sleep, and ignores the soft knocking at his door when Eleni swings by to check on him. Part of him regrets not opening up and pulling her inside, he could've easily propositioned her, and successfully if her demeanor towards him was being perceived accurately. He might've only been with two or three other people over the last years but he was good flirt, especially if there was alcohol but he didn't need it to initiate anything.

Still, sex could only solve certain problems for particular people.

For Dain it only ever left him feeling raw and exposed and there was nothing more he hated than feeling his skin against another person's and waking up alone and cold in the sheets afterwards. He could go for something strong and sweet though; mead or wine, anything could be fine. His Dad used to come home after patrols or duty irritated and would coerce him to drink with him, complaining about who did this or didn't do that, forcing Dain to listen and agree, pressing the issues so hot against his son's skin they'd eventually become Dain's too.

Currently he hides underneath his dark sheets from the sunlight that infiltrates his barracks room through the cushion-sized window. He's tired, like he had been returning from Draithus, only emotionally he recognizes that he's jaded from feeling to the extent that he'd allowed himself prior. He squeezes his pillow closer to him; it would nice if he could curl up against Cath, but his dragon hadn't spoke to him since his comment regarding Sgaeyl so he's made to imagine the feeling of smooth and warm red scales and the reverberating pulse behind it.

Skin or scale, he reckons.

He drowses off successfully again minutes later to more knocking; this time the wingleader rises to answer it.

"Good morning,"

To his bemusement Sloane Mairi stands at the threshold of his barracks room looking remarkably neat and distinguished, she tilts her head up to meet his despaired gaze and softens a bit. Dain ignores that, pretends he doesn't notice it, and also looks past the tray trapped in her grip,

"Cadet Mairi."

The sound of his own gravely voice makes Dain clear his throat; he tries again after swallowing and Sloane's eyes follow the rise and fall of his laryngeal prominence.

"Did you need me?" he frowns when his voice still husks out gruffly.

Sloane fidgets with the tray, and looks away, but then those pretty lakes are on him again.

"I have orders to bring you your meals because I failed to follow the initial order after your mending."

Dain wants to roll his eyes, "Who's orders?"

"Lieutenant Colonel Sorrengail's."

He presses the flat of his fingers against his beard and rubs, then steps aside; it's too early to argue.

"How long are the orders in effect for?" He mutters carefully as she unloads the food onto his table.

Sloane avoids answering until all of it, a total of three things and *bless it* a carafe of coffee, is neatly organized and off the tray,

"Until Cath stops requesting it."

Amari's sake, this fucking dragon. Can't answer a prod in their bond but he can still force a meal to appear.

Dain rounds the table and Sloane to slink back into his bed, "Don't do this again." he orders, "I'm not invalid, but thank you anyway."

"What happened to your back?"

Dain pauses; the first-year's words take seconds to piece against each other, but as they do we swivels around. It's too late, though, and Sloane's eyes are glimmering in the sunlight that shines perfectly against where she's chosen to stand,

"Did that happen because of me? During my Threshing?" her mouth gapes open, astonished, but she looks bothered by it.

"I'm fine." Dain mandates, unwilling to entertain the conversation and slipping back into his sheets. He pulls the flat sheet over his shoulders; there, done. Nothing to see.

"You got burned because of me?" Sloane cries out, "You had just walked away—I hadn't noticed you got hurt."

"Mairi, *stop.*"

Dain twists to face her, "It's not a fucking big deal, it healed, you didn't notice, it's fine."

The same way she hand't noticed he allowed Violet to tamper with her opponents during the days that were her designated matches. He'd gone as far as to arrange benefits for cadets to purposefully lose to her in the most believable ways possible when he and Violet were gone away in the Isles. Sloane would always got Archives Duty on days Jessinia was also on duty. She was never assigned to patrol with cadets Dain considered reckless or loyalists, inexperienced, or particularly hostile toward her. Even now, and he hadn't done so in the last fortnight, her weapons and protective equipment received more frequent inspections than she'd noticed; Sloane chocked that up to him being an annoying bastard more than anything probably.

She assumes a stance that is an uncanny cross between Violet and Imogen; bold and brazen, fists tight at her sides, and her sunlight strands pulled back into a neat bun. Her down-turned lips have a slight gap between them that makes Sloane look like she's perpetually pouting, but the heat in her eyes tells him otherwise.

"It's the length of your back Aetos; you never got it mended?"

"I wasn't authorized to receive mending for it once it was determined why it happened." he seethes, turning away again, "Thank you for breakfast, I'll eat it in a second. You're. Dismissed."

"When are you coming to class? It's only eight-hundred."

"I might not."

"You don't skip class."

"Mairi," Dain groans, "Get *out.*"

He doesn't dare roll to see her go, but he counts the seconds it takes her to shut the door, and then the minutes it takes him to lull back into sleep.
