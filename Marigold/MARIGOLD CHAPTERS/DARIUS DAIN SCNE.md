Dain can't help worrying for Andarna.

He can't *stop* once he starts.

She's mostly flightless, inable to carry her rider and shunned by her family for having the right intention but for the wrong reasons, at least according to them. He finds Cath is now frequently accompanied by her; she's never too far behind the older dragon, and occasionally drops in on Dain too but it makes him sick. He doesn't know what transpired between the Black dragon and his mate that caused her to absolve their mating bond, but Dain is well aware of the consequences that come from it and the worry for him and Violet is palpable in Andarna's body language.

*"She is in the company of those who only intend for best."* Cath softly states as Dain dismounts and retreats between his Red's ruby talons, *"You needn't worry for her."*

*"But how can I help her?"* Dain panics, eyes meeting Cath's marigold ones.

The Swordtail brushes against Dain in their bond, *"Help her human. It could be a month until Tairn pulls from his cycle of rest, and even then there is still physical rehabilitation. Their human is their only actual weakness during this time."*

Dain's head pitches up and drags down, bobbing away at the words he hears.
