Sloane sweeps the runed objects she'd brought to show Dain into a neat pile before cosildating them with the texts and other assignments she has neatly into her rucksack. Dain watches her, still taken by her thoughtfulness, and quietly stands ahead of her,

"Are you... nervous to go back to Basgaith? For the assessment?"

Sloane stares at him from his floor; her eyes have been a calm shade of blue that reminds him of runoff turned pond just near lakes after a storm. There's nothing unsure there, or at least not right now, in this moment. She takes in a deep breath, but it doesn't seem like the exasperation that escapes her is because of him this time.

"I am." Sloane begins, softly, not moving to make eye contact and instead playing with the inkpen he'd leant her, "But, more or less because our immediate future depends on my performance."

Dain feels his head bobbing, "Based on what I've observed in Draithus, I think you'll do mighty fine."

"You think I'll do fine because I'm a Mairi, you said that."

"Well, yes, but..." Dain squirms when their eyes meet; that's a given, but Sloane isn't just the prodigial child of a legend. Sloane is an exceptionally quick learner and even quicker to put her developed skill to practical use.

"You're insanely practical and efficient. I don't think that's something that can be taught overnight, epecially with the way you apply that displine to your methodology in wielding." He stops fighting the furious heat in Sloane's eyes and lets it melt him,

"These qualities will keep the people around you alive, unfortunately Basgaith doesn't exactly value how priceless that is, so let me be the one to tell you that I find you indispensible."

Sloane smiles, then grins, "For what your opinion is worth, you think I'm indispensible?"

"Yes. And I might not be worth--"

"No, don't start doing your Dain thing," Sloane groans, "You're worth plenty."

He snorts, "What makes you say that now?"

Sloane's eyes flicker around his face, searching him, "Thoirt likes Cath for reason's she won't say normally. But when it comes to his temperment and treatment of you there are comments." She lifts a hand in thought and crosses her legs over the other, "When it comes to Cath and his treatment of their peers, it's with the same favor."

Dain lets his feet move him closer to her. So close that she cranes her face upwards to look at him properly.

"So?" he remarks dryly, Cath is kind and gentle and has always been, but this might be a surprise to others.

"So, anyone who's dragon treats them even more gently than Thoirt does with me *must* be a person worthy." Sloane's eyes are *still* melting a hole through him, "And I know exactly how much I mean to her."

Dain grins this time, "You're giving me points because Thoirt likes Cath?"

"Points?"

"Yeah," Dain nearly stammers, "I don't know? Maybe you'll hate me a little less because Thoirt likes Cath and Cath likes me?"

Sloane's head tips to the side and all of the sunshine growing from her scalp tips with it, fine and dense and flowing, "You get points if I hate you less?"

"I didn't mean it literally. Stop overanalyzing it." He gripes, eyes rolling out of habit.

"How do I get points?" Sloane teases, uncrossing her legs and bending them instead so that she can rest her chin where her knees meet. Dain takes a few steps beck until his thighs collide with his table, then he grabs for a chair without taking his eyes off of the blonde. He sits down and thinks a moment.

"Why would you need points?"

Sloane rocks a bit, "I figured points would make you see *me* and not your regrets or guilt."

"I do see you, Mairi." Dain argues weakly.

"About as well as Violet does, Aetos, or as well as your father had ever seen you. Or Xaden with both of us." Her sapphire flame snuffs enough that his stomach gets tight, "Essentially like we were never there to begin with."

The comment feels like a slap.

Riorson had never known Dain, and had no interest in it either. But upon consideration Dain finds himself denying that he would've ever even given the other man the oppurtunity to get to know him. His father evidently had known Dain the least despite raising his son and dictating every aspect of his life, from dragon to potential wife and partners. And... Violet? He'd misunderstood and translated Violet all wrong. They didn't know each other anymore, which is why they're both trying to hard to understand one another now.

"You aren't close to Riorson?" Dain sqwuaks stupidly, "He and your brother were fostered together, right?"

Sloane throws her hands up and then lets them fall heavily at her sides; she pushes her face into her knees and groans so loudly it shocks Dain.

"I don't know Xaden outside of anything Liam has written to me, and even then it's mostly from the recent ones Violet had recovered from his room. The few times I was allowed to even see Liam, Xaden was off doing what in Poromiel. We aren't a jolly-good bunch just because those two were raised together." She spits out her story as if it tastes wrong in her own mouth, like it disgusts her as she hears it recited in her voice, "I'm an outsider in whatever narrative your mind writes up; promise you."

"...I didn't know that," Dain admits, desperate to understand Sloane's fury, "Do you not like him?"

"I don't *know* him enough to like or dislike him beyond understanding that he's the fucking reason I'm here in Basgaith in the first place." she sniffles and rubs her face into her knees, "I could've just stayed stupid and unwilling in Caldyr for all I cared after I found out he'd died."

"Did you like your foster home?"

Her eyes peak out from behind her hair when she lifts her face, "I *loved* that stupid old lady in comparison to being here."

That's all she gives, quiet and raging on the floor by his bed.

He's glad Sloane had been given a future with a person that she at least tolerated, by the sounds of it, "That's good. I'm sorry--"

"--No please, don't be; keep talking to me as if I'm one of your friends and not some tragic renegade baby who's fate you sealed."

"I'm glad Violet managed to save his letters for you," Dain declares, "I'm glad it was her. She's good about going against the grain."

Sloane unfolds and stands, fists balled tight, and teeth grit, "They aren't *her* letters to gatekeep, regardless of wether or not I fucking train, or fail out, though. Are they?" she points at Dain, "Did you ever think about that?"

"I--"

"And as sorry as you say you are you haven't even told me what the fuck happened that *caused* the whole situation that got him killed."

Dain gives her a puzzled look.

"I thought Violet would've--"

"--Violet would've what? Sat me down and explained a singular fucking thing to me? Dain was just doing what he thought was best, and it made your father empty out a whole fucking outpost to get rid of a few marked ones and her all at once. Great explanation, right?!"

Fuck.

He can't look at her like and feel okay because she's right. He doesn't know *anything* about the woman standing in front of him. But fuck if she doesn't sound just like him up until a few weeks ago; surrounded by people that don't actually know him and judge him based on what they've collected from others. She's just fucking alone and Dain isn't doing a damnned thing to help.

"Okay."

"That's it? Okay?" her body goes limp, "Figures."

"...are you tired?"

Sloane basically growls at him, "Trying to get rid of me?"

Dain pushes his hair back and watches her face go pink; he speaks quickly so he doesn't unintetionally upset her more, "We just flew for four hours, and we haven't properly showered in four days. I was going to ask if you wanted to do that and then come back?"

"We can't leave our floor unless permitted at this hour."

Shit, he forgot about that. Only leadership can come and go as they please.

"Right, I forgot; go to the showers on this floor and come back here. I'll get us some food, and then I'll shower and give you the explanation that you've had the right to."

"You *really* want to wash that badly?" Sloane prods, folding her arms across her her chest.

Dain only surrenders, "Cath smells so fucking bad sometimes, and I don't think he even made an attempt to wash at all because Aotrom and Ridoc kept fucking with everyone in the water. And he's molting soon, so his scales are squishy." his face falls into a disgusted grimace, "I hate feeling dirty."

He wants to wash away the feelings Sloane's brought over him, and the grime from their day all down the bathing chamber drains.

"Hungry too?" she ponders instead, with zero urgency.

"Yes Mairi, I'm hungry and dirty, and House staff closes the kitchen in less than hour, so please--"

"What do I wear after I shower?"

Dain gives her an incredulous look, "I have a few tunics, take your pick."

"I want the softest bed shirt you have." She demands.

"Whatever you want."

"And I need to smell your soap."

Dain sputters, irritation climbing up his neck and chipping away at his teeth, but he gets up and snatches his wash basket from where it sits on top of his personal trunk to the left of the armoire, "Here."

He watches her slender fingers carefully plucks his soap from where he'd set it on top of a new wash rag, she sniffs it and glances up at him.

"Fine. Please get me something light on the stomach, Wingleader."
