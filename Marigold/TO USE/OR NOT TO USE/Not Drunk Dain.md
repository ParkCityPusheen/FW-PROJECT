In life, most would agree that enemies lie in wait in the shadows, hidden amongst questionable intentions and across boundaries that a person prefers to stay behind. But, every so often, there lies the antlion larva in a funnel-shaped pit, buried within the depths of its own creation, waiting, while openly exposed. Patience in the face of a promised meal seals the fate of the larva's target, and the larva need not do a thing as its prey stumbles across the snare. Its victim struggles, unknowingly sinking into the pit, heedless of the larva beneath flicking sand upwards as they panic.

The whole scheme is meticulously designed to lure, confine, and destroy in a singular, unwitting step made by the larva's prey, right into their jaws.

And so, here lies Dain Aetos: Sloane's perfectly oblivious Wingleader, hopelessly descending (albeit tastefully) past woozy and into drunken stupor nearly against the shoulder of the unnecessarily large healer, Darius Fenryr. Who, for no fucking reason at all, pins Sloane with a gaze so sharp and ill-naturedly contemptuous, she considers asking him to sit next to Catriona Cordella so that she could properly grade the level of resting bitch face that she's been forced to endure in her leisure.

The fucking audacity of a *larva* and a pissy feline, Sloane thinks, nefariously.

Dain had been been seemingly happy to guzzle down whatever Fenryr had been flagging the barkeep for, meads and ciders, chatting with him, and Fenryr never lets Dain's attention waiver from his for too long. He's pushing the drinks ahead of the rider and distributing a casual touch, here, there.

What-fucking-*ever.* 

Cat sits pretty to Dain's left, tucked into the booth and cheekily reading the leather journal he's always scrawling away in at any given moment, her legs are crossed and she's got two empty glasses sticky against a third full one acquired at the same time Sloane had entered the pub and spotted the awkward gaggle. Sloane can tell that where Cat's legs are crossed, the thigh of the overlapping leg rests mostly against Dain's side, in a casual display of skinship that Sloane is only ever privy to during their training sessions.

Dain doesn't like to be touched. He said it himself, and shows it too; he wont even let his hands touch another person's as he passes along scrolls, books, and even runes. The harder it is to avoid touching, the more likely he is to drop whatever the item is just ahead of the receiving individual. But he's here, face pinking, teeth shining in sconce lighting, looking groomed and well-to-do, even as he gets intoxicated by Mr. Moneybags-Fenryr who flags the barkeep again to bring Dain more honey-yellow mead. She's certain as Amari is Queen, that Dain grumbles something about Fenryr not needing to buy him another drink and the healer laughs in his face with skin like perfectly fucking polished bronze in the warm lighting, and tilts his head to the side,

"Not at all an issue when it's for you, pretty boy."

And proximity be damned, his arm swims under the table to rest against Dain's thigh, and the rider only chuckles again. Fenryr's hand does eventually drop, but only after Dain lifts his glass to his mouth, which could either be a very gracious rejection, or Fenryr has enough sense to move so that it isn't an awkward motion for him make. Had this been anyone else, they would've parted instantaneously from her Wingleader's leg after he burned them with a gaze containing the heat of his dragon's fire.

"You gonna go in or is it that busy, Sloane?"

Aaric.

Sloane's eyes peel away from Dain and his troupe, and over her shoulder to her year mate,

"You know you can walk around me, right?"

Aaric's whole body pivots forward in obvious offense, "So then, am *I* choosing where we're going to sit?" A group of officers shuffle past them, forcing Sloane and Aaric press up against the half-wall she was spying at Dain over, "Is there a fight? I've standing here for like three minutes."

His eyes find Dain as quickly as hers had, and Aaric's reaction seems just as shocked as hers was.

"The fuck is he doing."
