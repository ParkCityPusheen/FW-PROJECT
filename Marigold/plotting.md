# Storyboard / Plotting — Working Title TBD

## North Star
A tumultuous love story centered on rider/dragon intimacy (Dain & Cath), with a slow-burn Dain/Sloane romance. Dain must face his mistakes before others are allowed to see him as more than them. Liberation and mutual first-love are the emotional destination of the longer work; Arc 1 ends closer to graduation with that future opened, not finished.

## Thematic Axis (Love / Life against War / Death)
Metaphor only. The gods are real in-world; the riders are not them and will not become them. Couples as mirror images: opposite, but the same. Violet/Xaden ~ war + death (destructive power embraced as a tool for good). Dain/Sloane ~ love + life (siphon feared as killing, shown as saving). This fic lives on the Love/Life side. Dain’s caregiving and Sloane’s terror of hurting people are the counter-force, not a softer subplot. Family / Loysam lore later; do not invent. Full working note: `Marigold Theory Incorperation Guide.md`.

## What This Fic Is About
- Rider/dragon relations as the emotional engine (especially Dain/Cath)
- Mental health across quadrants; the weight of expectation from peers and superiors
- Usefulness, powerlessness, and the search for other paths (runes / study / flier-adjacent alliances)
- Where Dain fits when the Wingleader body is not enough: healer orbit, awkward agreeance with fliers (especially Poromiel immigrants), civilian labor as chosen usefulness
- Facing harm done before repair is earned
- Slow-burn attachment that becomes impossible to dismiss

## Core Relationships

### Dain & Cath (primary)
- Surface: Cath reads gentle, accepting, dry, sardonic
- Reality: brutish and controlling; that control is fondness and fear of loss wearing armor
- Cath would never say he loves Dain; he proves it by refusing to let Dain destroy himself or quit into nothing
- **Prior rider wound (no Cath POV):** Cath once gave counsel; that rider was lost to their own mind. Because of that, Cath now refuses to offer advice. He contains, orders, deflects, stays present. He does not guide.
- Cath recognizes the *signs* of what humans call depression in Dain without naming it or understanding it as such; his fear is cindering and specific: losing another rider to the inside of their own head
- Dain feels this only as hints: bond-tightening, disproportionate control, abrupt topic-kills, heat/fear spikes Cath won’t explain, dialogue that protects instead of advises
- Later (Sloane era): Cath may still be sarcastic and observational, but it should read as commentary/containment, not the kind of counsel he swore off after the last rider

### Dain & Sloane (romantic slow burn)
- Begins in childish banter / military hierarchy / business
- Deepens through runes study and Dain’s pull toward usefulness beyond his signet’s limits
- Sloane grows curious against her will; falls deeper while resisting the idea of him
- Dain recognizes his feelings openly and comes to terms fast (with Cath’s commentary)

### Dain & Violet (repair, delayed)
- First: Dain pulls away almost entirely
- Much later: drifts back into proximity/repair because of Riorson (tracking / aftermath), not because he forces a reunion early

### Dain & Catriona (unlikely friendship)
- Ch 1 starts hostile: she wants him irate; he calls her a conniving outsider; leadership lecture vs withdrawal. Friendship is not prewritten from that scene.
- Spark (Phase B hinge / Ch 2 candidate): Dain and Cath volunteer with flier groups to organize and relocate Poromiel immigrants. Fliers instantly appreciate his concern; equally wonder why he chooses the gryphon-bonded and their people over his own. His honest answers to that question prompt the friendship.
- Unlikeliness is the point. Spark in the volunteer work; deepen across Phase B. Do not rush warmth.
- Marin and other flier-adjacent closeness can follow this door; Cat is the first unlikely one.

### Dain & Rider counterparts (Cat, Marin, etc.)
- As Dain looks for paths that make him deadly/useful, he grows close to Cat, Marin, and other rider/flier-adjacent figures
- Runes + Sloane’s knowledge/study become a concrete outlet for that hunger
- Healer orbit (Darius, Kaia, medical labor) is a parallel search for fit: care work that is not command

## Cast (priority)

Major recurring: Dain’s father, Catriona, Marin, Violet, Ridoc, Violet’s squad broadly, primarily-mentioned canon fliers
Supporting: possible OCs as needed; keep them in orbit, not center

## Phase A — Aftermath & Withdrawal
**Job:** Stage Dain’s mental deterioration and Cath’s cindering fear of losing another rider to his own mind. Seed withdrawal. Do not solve anything yet.

### Dain’s spiral (why the foundation is rotting)
- Everything he built his life on is a decayed foundation (institution, loyalty, “right thing,” father’s world)
- Outside Basgiath leadership roles, he feels more-or-less useless
- Friendless and lonely
- Experiences himself as hated: not the whole world, but Violet’s squad and Sloane are so loudly abhorrent he cannot get past it; Liam’s death sits inside that hatred as permanent proof
- Future looks bleak: front lines likely; father has entirely disowned him; shit standing with the only family left means no soft landing
- **BIG SEED (Ch 1 theatre / Royce beat):** Loneliness as function, not solitude. Not empty-room loneliness. He is surrounded (Darius, Royce, the theatre, the Wing) and he is working: troubleshooting missing riders, refusing to chase Violet’s orbit, reassigning people to jobs he already assigned. None of that makes him a person in the room. He is a function. A body with rank. Royce reports to the title. Second Squad does not answer to the man. Violet’s orbit does not include him. So he sits on the dais doing the correct next mechanical thing while wanting something leadership cannot give him. The Violet fantasy is not really about Violet; it is about belonging and agency: being trusted, mattering as more than a body with a title, choosing a rule to break because he chose to. Under the resentment and duty-performance, the real feeling is: *I am alone inside this role, and I am starting to want more than the role.* That wanting-more is a major growth engine for the whole fic (feeds Phase B usefulness/paths; do not solve in Ch 1, only feel it).

### Cath in this phase (reader gets no POV)
- Fear: Dain is sliding the same way the last rider did
- Behavior: less advice, more containment; fondness wearing as control
- Dain misreads some of it (irritation, pride, dragon-aloofness) while still *feeling* the wrongness in the bond
- **Theatre/Royce BIG FEELINGS beat:** Cath clocks the loneliness/wanting-more as large and dangerous. He cannot help his rider through it. He can only watch Dain go through it. Stamp/containment (*None of that. Tairn…* / *focus on the task*) is what he has instead of aid. At times like this he is only just a dragon. No Cath POV: show this as bond heat that offers presence and refusal, not a path forward.
