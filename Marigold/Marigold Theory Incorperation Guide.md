# Couple Mirror Axis (Marigold long-game theme)

Working adoption of a RY-adjacent reading. Couples as mirror images: opposite, but the same. Destinies intertwined; alternative routes. Family / Sgaard / Loysam details stay blank until built. Do not invent them.

**Hard rule:** Dunne, Loial, Malek, and Amari are the in-world gods. The four riders are not those gods, will not become those gods, and are not divine vessels. The pairing is metaphor only: a thematic rhyme for coaching and long-game shape. On the page they stay human. Let temperament, care, and signet-fear carry the theme. In-world prayer, temples, and Isles remain ordinary devotion, not evidence of becoming.

**The shared mechanism:** one partner helps the other stop treating a feared or unwanted power as only destruction, and see it as a tool that can protect. Xaden does this for Violet’s lightning. Dain does this for Sloane’s siphon. Same ritual, opposite charge.

**Violet + Xaden ~ War + Death (metaphor)**
- Violet ~ Dunne: lives in war and power whether she wants it. Destructive signet she did not want. Xaden helps her focus it, embrace it, use it for good.
- Xaden ~ Malek: lives close to death. Brings it, accepts it; soul dying from venin. War and death go hand in hand.

**Dain + Sloane ~ Love + Life (metaphor)**
- Dain ~ Loial: steadier and calmer than the death-culture around him. Seeks safety; wants people not to get hurt. Caregiving, gentleness, even coddling. After Threshing he sits diametrically opposite Violet-the-violent. Love, like war, changes people forever (lifetime dedication lore is world texture, not a path to godhood). Isle of Loysam is a later door, not a current plot lock. Background he may not even know exists: later, not now.
- Sloane ~ Amari: life against Xaden’s death-rhyme. Forced into the quadrant; talks a tough line she does not fully back. Temper, chips, nightmare youth, no family, crush on Dain. Terrified her siphon will kill or hurt; Dain shows her it can save, and uses himself to prove it.

**Marigold’s job:** this fic lives on the Love / Life side of that mirror. Dain’s healer orbit, chosen usefulness, Cath intimacy, and slow-burn with Sloane are not a side romance. They are the human counter-force to war and death. Cath’s refusal to lose another rider to the inside of his own head belongs here as life-preserving pressure, not as a third god-pairing.

**Do not build yet:** Sgaard / maternal family; Loysam on-page; what Dain does not know about his background; lifetime dedication as plot machinery.
