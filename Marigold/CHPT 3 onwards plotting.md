| Stage              | Dain's approach                                                                                               |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Understand**  | Have Sloane recount both known uses in exact detail.                                                          |
| **2. Research**    | Search historical records for siphons and unusual signet behavior.                                            |
| **3. Identify**    | Determine what triggers her signet and what the magic actually feels like.                                    |
| **4. Control**     | Practice activating and **stopping** the signet deliberately.                                                 |
| **5. Isolate**     | Test one variable at a time: source, amount, distance, duration, target.                                      |
| **6. Transfer**    | Test **source → Sloane**, then **Sloane → target**, then whether she can move magic directly between sources. |
| **7. Limits**      | Establish how much she can take/give, how long she can sustain it, and what happens when she pushes too far.  |
| **8. Application** | Only once she understands the mechanics: practical/combat uses.                                               |
| **Core principle** | **Don't teach her what her signet does; teach her how to discover what it does.**                             |


### Late-Night Training

- Sloane realizes Dain is more tired/less argumentative late at night.
- She deliberately schedules training later and stretches sessions out.
- She starts exploiting his exhaustion to get more access to him.

### First Bed Incident

- Sloane showers and returns wearing Dain's shirt.
- She gets into his bed despite his objections.
- Dain eventually gets in too out of sheer defiance.
- Sloane gradually presses closer; Dain becomes visibly/physically flustered.

### The Game

- Sloane realizes proximity makes Dain weak.
- She deliberately uses harmless skinship: shoulder-to-shoulder, leaning against him, staying close.
- She teases him about reacting while he insists she's imagining it.
- Dain increasingly looks forward to their late-night sessions despite himself.

### Other Intimate Moments

- **Form correction:** Dain has to physically adjust her stance/movements.
- **Post-training:** exhausted silence → Sloane leans against him → he doesn't move away.
- **Minor injury:** Dain becomes unexpectedly gentle and attentive.
- **Stolen clothes:** Sloane repeatedly "borrows" his shirts/jacket.
- **Falling asleep:** Sloane sees Dain completely unguarded and relaxed.
- **Core dynamic:** Darius receives Dain's voluntary vulnerability; Sloane starts finding ways to access his vulnerability through proximity and exhaustion.  
  
1. Curiosity
  - She starts noticing things about Dain she hadn't before: his physical capability, intelligence, gentleness, and especially how different he becomes around Darius.
  - **Thought:** *Since when is Dain like this?*
  ### 2. Comparison
  - She notices Darius gets a version of Dain that nobody else seems to see—shy, receptive, vulnerable.
  - **Thought:** *Why does he let Darius get that from him? Why can't I?*
  ### 3. Experimentation
  - She discovers Dain is much less guarded when tired.
  - She starts staying later, partly to see whether she can draw out that version of him.
  - **Thought:** *What happens if I stay? What can I get him to show me?*
  ### 4. Discovery
  - She realizes physical proximity affects him more than she expected.
  - Dain becomes stiff, quiet, flustered, or loses his train of thought.
  - **Thought:** *Oh. I actually make him nervous.*
  ### 5. Enjoyment
  - Once she knows she can affect him, she starts doing it deliberately.
  - The closeness becomes fun—especially because Dain is otherwise so difficult to rattle.
  - **Thought:** *He can intimidate everyone else. But I can make him lose his composure.*
  ### 6. Possessiveness / wanting access
  - The comparison with Darius starts bothering her less as jealousy and more as **wanting the same emotional access**.
  - She wants to know what Dain is like when he stops guarding himself with *her*.
  - **Thought:** *If he can let Darius see him like that, I want to know if I can get there too.*
  ### 7. Emotional realization
  - Eventually she realizes she isn't just interested in making him flustered.
  - She genuinely likes being close to him and begins seeking the intimacy for its own sake.
  - **Thought:** *I don't just want to see him vulnerable. I want him to be vulnerable with me.*
  ### The progression in one line:
  **"Why is Dain like this?" → "Why does Darius get this version of him?" → "Can I get that version too?" → "Oh, I can make him flustered." → "This is fun." → "I actually want to be close to him."**
