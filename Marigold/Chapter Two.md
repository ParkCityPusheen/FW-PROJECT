Riorson House is truly an architectural feature of its time.

Dain, easily impressed by man-made structures of all types, stands in obvious awe of the double staircase that yawns ahead of him at the end of the Great Hall. Each leveled landing is staggered further into the stone edifice, sinking backwards as if falling away from him as he stands before it. The entire composition of the staircase is carved directly into and out of the stone mountain that's been whittled away to reveal the fortress in all it's grandeur. He can't help but compare it to Basgiath, both locations contain arrangements worth dropping a jaw at.

He runs his hand along the thick stone bannister's top, fingers stuttering over the Tyrrish tile fitted into the ends of each railing, another subtle detail worth noticing. He wonders what parts of the fortress he might not be allowed in, considering it houses this obelisk of a staircase, and what those areas might look like.

He hasn't even properly flown over Riorson House to see it from above because he fears being casually skyborne with Cath for too long without reason might be cause for concern to consider him suspicious to anyone here in ill-standing with Navarre.

Which is everyone, probably.

*"If you would like to see it you may. We can take to the skies now. No human will question me where I wish to, nor when I shall fly—and certainly not why."*

The Red's words are filled with spite and aggitation, Cath's emotions swell in Dain's mind while the smell of breakfast kicks up an entirely seperate set of senses while his nostrils flare dragging it in with a greedy sniff. He hasn't eaten in over twenty-four hours, which isn't an epidemic or life threatening, but the smell of eggs and butter-soaked honey biscuits make his belly cramp in anticipation.

It's early enough that it's not odd for him to be eating, but usually he'd be knicking names off his mental checklist of cadets within his Wing. He considers doing just that, exiting out of the Aretian Fortress to round it's flank and up to the valley above where cadets have been ordered to muster prior to eating—but he has Squad and Section Leaders to collect reports from to avoid doing it himself.

Dain looks back up the double polished staircase, barely noticing the diaphanous light filtering from the earliest hint of dawn through the collection of small windows trailing adject to the ascending steps. He isn't *needed* to make sure perfectly capable adults wake themselves up and find themselves accounted for where they know they should be, so he shuffles haggardly towards the Mess Hall.

*"Correct choice, Hatchling."* Cath comments, tersely, *"Eat, then come to me."*

Dain's eyes meet several other's belonging to a group of veteran Aretian riders, one gives him a thumbs up and the Wingleader exchanges a grimaced smile,

*"Where are you?"*

*"Valley. Watching your humans trickle in."* the swordtail drawls disdainful.

Breakfast provided is an assortment of light meats from several flightless birds and their eggs, sauteed vegetables, and seasonal fruits harvested from local farms. Dain waits in line, fills his plate with the brownish scrambled yolks from a Tyrrish Flute bird and seasoned roots with sweet peppers. He helps himself to two biscuits, the biggest he could find of the bunch, and a cup of coffee with some milk. He gives his name to the treasurer at the end of the buffet styled line and finds a seat at a smaller table meant only for a party of three or four along the outskirts of the Mess Hall.

His first sip of coffee is hot along the roof of his mouth and tongue and goes down easy; he feels insouciant and easy, shoving the fluffy Tyrrish honey biscuits into his mouth right after the coffee and then the eggs as he begins swallowing the thick dough.

*"Slow down,"* Cath spits out, acerbic tone even more unimpressed than usual.

Dain groans, but he squints with regret when the sound slips out more akin to a moan and he washes it down with another mouthful of Aretian apline brew, *"I'm hungry."* he responds stupidly, with a smile ghosting across his features.

*"I can see and hear that."*

*"I want to be there before the professors get out there,"* Dain admits, hovering a forkful of eggs and bird and peppers,

*"You will be, at the rate you refuse to chew your food,"* there's a sudden silence, then Cath adds, *"There is a human here, looking for you. The one with the roped hair."*

*"They aren't ropes, they're braids, Cath."* Dain answers, tentative and assuming, *"Matthias?"*

*"No, not Fierge's, the one in blue."*

Ropes? Not braids. Locs, then; so Darius, Dain thinks. He pictures the cocoa-skinned healer, draped in minty-blue robes,

*"Yes, that one. Darius."*

Oh.

Dain scrapes his food around on his plate, *"Wonder what he wants."*

*"He only asks your whereabouts, not what he wants with you."*

*"Has anyone asked him why?"*

Cath takes a moment, then: *"Yes the Little Lioness."*

Dain rolls his eyes; Cath has a strange liking of Sloane Mairi and he can't place why—probably because she fixed him good in the jaw and his rider ate it like his breakfast because the occasion wasn't appropriate to fucking correct her at the time.

When he doesn't say anything Cath continues leisurely: 

*"Seems he has no interest in placating her curiosity."*

Meaning Darius probably ignored her or said something smart right back to her like he had yesterday.

*"Sounds exciting."* Dain scoops up the rest of his eggs, feigning interest, but Cath doesn't respond anyways.

The rider finishes his food first, then takes his time draining the rest of his coffee. He considers finding time to sneak away into town after realizing he doesn't have a thermos that could grant him a second or third or fourth cup depending on the size he purchases. It would be nice, to wash away misery with a scalding cup of coffee in any instance. The thought prompts another as he stands up, carrying his tray of food to the staff on the opposite side of the food, handing it gently to the woman half the size of him, eyes crinkling at the corners when she smiles up at him in greeting.

Dain's not sure he even has the funds to purchase a thermos for hot liquids. He thinks of steel and ceramic, then their prices. Momentarily he experiences the embarrassment of not having the funds, but it dissipates into irritation acknowledging exactly *why* he's got about a hope and a dream worth of funds.

Fucking Basgiath. Navarre. His father.

One inevitably equates to the other since he'd split the quadrant, and never in good regard when it came to his personal life. When his father disowned him after his return from the Isles it had become alarmingly clear that he'd done all that he'd needed to to make sure Dain understood exactly how dead he was to the man.

Dain Aetos had been removed and disinherited from the Aetos family registry entirely, as if he'd ceased to exist at all. His access to the household treasury had been revoked, along with all the money accumulating during his active service at Basgiath; two-hundred gold pieces is what Dain maintained in currency and it was only because he'd planned on buying some extra uniforms with it after graduating.

He's not sure what the result of continuing to eat without a treasury assigned in his name will be, either. Maybe he'll recieve a bill alongside his officer rank during graduation, totaling the expense of every combined meal since his disownment. He'll feel every drop of coffee he'd ingested in the form of an indebted punch to the teeth.

It would be simple to travel out Riorson House and into the city it overlooks and establish himself with a merchant house, but he'd need a steep deposit to do even that, *especially* as a Navarrian citizen in Tyrrendor; only the Tyrrish weren't additionally taxed to trust income, loans, and savings within Tyrrish Banks. In addition to the deposit he'd need the time, and there were hardly enough hours in the day for him to sleep without being questioned for it.

Money troubles is one thing, and then theres his legitmacy as a literal person. He was meant to inherit certain of his father's properties, intended at using his name, wearing their crest, parading it as he climbed in rank——but Gods be damnned, Dain didn't even want to serve at this point, and he would fucking stop if it weren't for dragons sharing a mutual enemy with humankind.

He needs to eradicate all venin before he can disappear into a forest somewhere and live unbothered and unknown. But, for now, without an associated merchant house or household treasury, the funds he's actively earning while risking his life at this fucking war-machine of a college are entirely free, with nowhere to be stored.

Despite the raging stream-of-consciousness rolling in his thoughts, Dain swaggers to the valley in percieved equanimity, eyes lowered to the floor, and arms swinging by his side.

Dain grins psychotically to himself, how much more should he silently accept before he draws the line? He hopes he looks fine, because everything is far from it.

Dain arrives with impeccable timing at the base of the valley.

He wordlessly receives the paper muster-reports from each of his Section Leaders; each contain their Squad Leader's reports, together a cohesive attendance of the whole Wing to be delivered by himself to the superior awaiting them.

Said superior is part of the Assembly, and now their rune proffessor, accompanied by Professor Devera.

"Aetos. You're looking better." she glances over her shoulder at Cath up the hill, "Your dragon's been watching over the Wings."

"He's curious in nature." is all Dain says, still too perturbed by his ruminating earlier for posturing.

He does look with her though, at Cath, he's sitting on his haunches, wings tucked neatly, and his injured one not as flush against his body as the other. The two make eye contact and Cath stands up, capricious as ever, stalking closer to Dain and shaking everyone in the Wing while doing so,

*"The healer is there."* he says, tail flipping out from behind him and pointing at Darius awkwardly across all three other Wings. He's got a basket.

Trissa and Devera's heads also follow where Cath's tail stabs towards, eyes landing on the healer just as Dain's do; the movement is to sudden and Cath is too fucking red and large to not acknowledge,

"He's been looking for you." Trissa mentions, when her eyes befall light-blue robes across the valley, "There's only a few announcements this morning, go to him; healers mean you're hurt, and we need leadership in their best shape especially now."

Dain nods, but he goes to Cath first, and together (ridiculously) they make their way to Darius. Thankfully he meets them halfway, so Cath only deafens the whole student body for a total of about ten of his own steps. Darius stands his ground when he's close enough to yell at Dain, situated beneath Cath's chest,

"Can you come out from there?" he barks, lifting the hand carrying the woven basket, "Got a delivery for you!"

Dain tips his head back, eyes grazing over the cales at Cath's underside. The scales along his belly are an ethereal garnet, so dark at night they seem black, but in the first shining light of the civil dawn a deep, expensive looking ruby. From here all he can make out are scales, the dragon's body eclipses everything else.

*"Go, Hatchling."* Cath suggests, neck snakishly swinging out to attempt a glance at Dain near his forelegs,

*"Moving."*

When he reaches Darius, the basket is unceremoniously shoved in his direction and Dain searches the healer's face for signs of concern. There's none, just a goofy grin and shining ivory teeth against ebony skin,

"Tsk, Tsk, Dain." Darius states instead, imperturbable and calm, "You didn't stop at pharmacy like you were supposed to."

Kaia *had* told him to stop there, but he'd bumrushed out of Brennan's office to escape Violet's presence.

"Shit," he exclaims, scratching the side of his cheek, "I completely forgot, sorry."

Darius shruggs, "That's fine, I've got some stuff for you, might take the edge off after the mending."

He's presented with a paper box filled with dried tea leaves in portioned packets, two ointments that smell wonderfully floral, and three specially made cud-rods, "Cud-rods?" Dain mutters, picking them up and rolling them in his hand. There are also small vials with topical oils and a collection of vitamins and supplements all neatly labeled with Darius' large handwriting.

"Yeah, you chew em' and they microdose you with painkillers, keeps the whole body numb in fair moderation." he responds nonchalantly.

Dain's eyes narrow, "Fair moderation?"

"Absolutely do not get caught with these, especially by someone who understands propietery herbal practices. My family used to make them for my uncle regularly—he was terminally ill, but wanted to live so... pain-free is the best we could do for him."

"I don't think—"

"At night, at the very least, have a chew. They're good for weeks, toss them when the taste fades out. Keep your nerves calm." Darius adds, not giving the rider the chance to deny him.

"Okay, alright, fine. I'll keep them." Dain looks at the rods once more and then back at Darius, "You aren't planning on sabotaging me with this are you?"

"No, man." the healer's eyes bounce up Dain's body, probably assessing his shape since seeing Brennan,

"Just like seeing you, you seem like a good guy."

Dain blinks, surprised, but not taken aback, "Oh, okay." He senses a bit of apprehension in Darius's body langauge and doubles down on the interaction, 

"Thanks. It’s comforting, having someone think of me. Not sure what to say really."

Darius lights up instantly, "Come out on the town with someof us sometime. You'd like my friends."

"But would your friends like me?" Dain chuckles, kicking himself inwardly at the knee-jerk response.

"Of course they would, you're easy on the eyes, probably easy to get on with too—when you're not all wound up with this 'dragon, kill, kill, kill,' shit."

*"Oh?"* Cath coos.

Darius maintains their eye contact, ducking down a bit to catch Dain's eyes once more when he begins to look away.

"...that could be nice."

Darius smiles again, "Nice? That's all, sheesh."

"No, I mean—look," Dain whips his head around, feeling oddly out of place, "I don't get invited out, or like included in things, so I'm just—"

"Shy? Wasn't expecting that from you. Kinda like it." He fucking winks and shoves the basket into Dain's hands,

"I think you just haven't met your match. You should see the way that little first-year gets all on her toes about you. Fiesty little thing."

Dain finally smiles back, "She hates me."

"Mhmm, no I don't think so." Darius laments, brows jumping, "It's like you know exactly who I'm talking about and I don't even know her name to mention it."

"I promise you: she *hates* me. She just wants in on it if you're trying to kill me."

Dain holds up the basket suggestively and Darius shakes his head,

"I'll see you around then, yeah?"

Dain shrugs, "If you want."

"Oh yeah, *I* want." Darius drawls before sauntering off and pulling the hood of his robes up.

Dain's mouth drops open, but it's Cath who speaks,

*"And here I assumed your celibacy would never end."*

*"Who said this was mutual?"* Dain snarks, whipping around to face Cath.

*"You could use a distraction."*

*"I never thought you'd be the permiscuous type."*

*"And you should continue to avoid mingling in your elders' business."*

Dain snaps his mouth shut and walks past his dragon, falling back into his position at the head of his Wing; arguing with Cath really isn't on the agenda for today.

Once he's situated, stated straight, but not at attention, and Cath ascending the valley to a much further point where he can observe with ennui, Dain empties his mind and absorbs the informtation Trissa begins to broadcast across the Wings.

"In light of recent events the academic calander will pause until more stable grounds have been established. During this time there will be a skeleton of classes and assignments to maintain order, and as of today those classes include: weapons training, flight lessons and tactis, and rune weaving."

Trissa allows a passing minute of silence, waiting for the whispering to die down, then carries on,

"In the next four weeks specifically, sections from each Wing will rotate between Basgiath and Aretia, the beginning of each week tasks and schedules will be given out and you are all expected to adhere to them as per General Aetos." 

*"Fuck that guy."* Dain bites out into the bond, with perfunctory bitterness.

*"Yes."* Cath adds, his derision entirely honest.

"Wingleaders will assign the aforementioned tasks thereafter."

Dain almost groans outloud and just barely manages to keep from dramatically rolling his head along his shoulders, *"Great."*

Cath raises his Wings beyond Trissa and Devera, *"It could be."*

This week First and Third Wing are expected to fly back to Basgiath if they are in good enough health, while Second and Fourth will remain in Aretia to help Riorson House staff and nearby farmers. There are handfuls that will be pulled away, squads at a time, to participate in collecting medicinal herbs and aid medical units. Between it all, Dain is still expected to assign patrols, watches, and collect and review reports (turned in by Squad and Section Leaders regarding their finished tasks), and then to procure his own.

"Wingleaders should observe their Wings at the best of their ability throughout this time, we expect you all to preserve a regular order by whatever means you deem necessary." Devera finishes, "Your professors will all be either in the theatre, teaching one of the supplementary courses, or in the office directly across the Mess Hall. Maintain use of your chain of command, as per usual."

When Devera and Trissa leave, they do so after handing out cloned scrolls with the skeleton schedule for the classes, and the other mentioned tasking, to each Wingleader. Cath creeps closer, just a few dozen meters behind Dain, while his Wing inches towards him, watching with expectant eyes.

Dain's mind clicks, puttering into gear before he speaks.

Second Wing files in closer as well, Eleni Jareth and her Executive Officer, Tyvon Varen, huddle nearby him while the cadets mix into his Wing,

"Sup, Aetos." Jareth perks up, clapping him against his shoulder, "Wanna rub our heads together for this?"

Dain's eyes swing from Jareth to Varen, both seem to bear no malicious intent, but Dain glances up and over to Cath where four outher dragons have joined him,

"You want to?" He echoes, expecting a snide remark.

Varen nods excitedly, "The proffesors never complain about Fourth Wing's status. Even while you were gone, it ran itself."

Dain attributes that feat to being a ruthlessly unrelenting bastard when anyone crossed him pointlessly, and being as helpful as he humanly could when they were the same for him. Servant leadership, something his father never believed in.

"I don't mind. I don't have an Executive officer so it'd be nice."

The pair beam, smiling cheek-to-cheek at his resolve, "I've never had an issue with you, and I look forward to being here in Aretia with you." Jareth lilts, pushing out her to shake his own.

He shakes hers and her EO's, then turns to face the Wings,

"Morning." He breathes, dispassionate and expertly masking the trepidation he feels at the amount of authority and liabilty that's been handed to him. The greeting is returned as unenthusiastically as he's given it, and in other pockets, more motivated.

Jareth waves to her Wing, they wave back.

Dain's eyes find Violet, and hers find him through the crowd of bodies. There's over one hundred and eighty riders present, and eighty fliers in the combined Wings. One being Catriona, who peers at him through squished eyes and long lashes.

There's a nuetral murmuring happening around them, Dain simply speaks over it, looking past Violet, and projecting his voice over the student body with lesser magic,

"We don't have a time squeeze currently, so I'll make rounds to each squad with a watchbill for patrols and watch stations by tonight." he dips his head to Jareth.

"We'll be working in tandem with Fourth Wing, no point in making this any harder than it needs to be."

"Power in numbers," Varen adds.

He waits for the collective groan, and there it is. Dain smiles empathetically.

"Today Claw Section, first squad will be assisting Riorson House staff until dismissed, Tail Section second squad with the agricultural focuses; you can go ahead and muster up with the veteran riders at the courtyard, apparently more directives will be put out by them, there."

Dain watches that squad file out, then drones on, "Flame Section, third squad for medicinal herbs..." he drops his head down to the scroll, "...mustering at the peak northbound past the valley, Proffessor Kaori and some healers are there to intercept you at the redevous point." 

Likewise, Jareth mimics his assignments but in the opposite order, starting with Flame Section, first squad and down the line,

"In the meantime we've been assigned those three classes, starting with flight lessons, right here in the valley—standby for whoever comes in—"

Varen peaks over Jareth's shoulder, "There's no professor assigned?"

"Nope, just 'to be determined' for the whole week." She grunts.

Varen clicks his tongue and she finishes addressing the Wings,

"Weapons training are a half hour after that, we'll break for an hour long lunch, and then rune weaving is last. Battle Brief Saturday morning in the theatre."

Dain nods, "Got that?"

He hears the collective "Yes Sir," from his Wing and rolls the scroll back up, tucking it away into his pocket,

"We'll rotate the Aretian tasks clockwise by one section and sqaud. Meaning: tomorrow Riorson House staff will be assisted by Tail Section, third squad."

Another murmur of understanding leaves Dain satisfied and ready to step away, but a voice cracks through the air like a crossbolt,

"You know why your Dad implemented this weird schedule?"

Dain looks over his shoulder, then back, "Who's dad?"

The cadet in question points at Dain, one of his own from Claw section, "Yours, Aetos."

"Look, I've been completely disinherited. We don't speak, nor do I want to. We're strangers and have never been anything more." His answer parts from his lips lacking any contrition and almost completely full of disaffection. He's shocked to see Violet looking hurt by his answer, and he can't quite place why that'd be her reaction considering how much she hates his father now.

Either way it's all irrelevant.

"Oh, well," the cadet adds, "I was just wondering."

Dain looks away from him, "Muster up in thirty for flight lessons. You're all dismissed."
