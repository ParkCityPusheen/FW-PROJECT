# Writing Guidelines

Marigold editing, beta reading, and revision are governed by the Marigold rules in this folder:

- `marigold-philosophy.mdc`
- `marigold-beta-reading.mdc`
- `marigold-voice-dialogue.mdc`
- `marigold-edits-and-continuity.mdc`

Those rules apply whenever the work is Marigold. Do not simplify the prose, invent missing events, or rewrite a passage unless the author explicitly asks for that rewrite.

The Empyrean vault stays opt-in in `empyrean-canon-on-request.mdc`.
